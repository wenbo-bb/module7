Greenhouse Gas Emissions per Person and Population

Data from Jones et al. (2025), Our World in Data (2024, 2025), and United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Population Division (UN, 2024).

Practice summary

Use a double-panel diagram to compare the per capita emissions and population of Brazil, China and India from 2000 to 2023. Conclusion: India's population exceeds that of China, but per capita emissions are still the lowest.

Step:

Fixed variables: year, country, per capita emissions, population.

Structure: The upper and lower panels share the X axis (year).

Reserve data: the unified population unit is one million, and there is no missing value.

Drawing: Panel A per capita emissions, Panel B population, distinguish countries by color and line.

Make a card: write the title, summary, source, coding description and limitations.

Check: Check that the label, source and card information are consistent.

Data Visualisation Data Card

Title

The population of India exceeds that of China, but the per capita emissions are still lower than that of China.
Summary

The two panels compared the per capita emissions and total population of Brazil, China and India from 2000 to 2023 respectively. In 2023, India has the largest population and the lowest per capita emissions.
